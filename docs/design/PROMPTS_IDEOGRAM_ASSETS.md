# Prompts para assets finais no Ideogram

Gerar cada item separadamente. Não pedir ao Ideogram para escrever textos longos nem desenhar a interface do produto.

## 1. Símbolo e ícone PWA

```text
Create a production-ready app icon for PrescriMed, a fast Brazilian medical prescription web app. Design one simple geometric monogram that subtly combines the letter P with the Rx concept. Flat vector style, distinctive at 24 px, centered within a 70 percent safe area, balanced negative space, deep navy to clinical blue background, white symbol with one restrained emerald accent. Opaque square background, no text, no words, no UI, no dashboard, no mockup, no device, no medical cross, no shield, no heartbeat line, no stethoscope, no pills, no photorealism, no gradients that reduce small-size legibility. 1024x1024.
```

Escolher uma única variação. Depois exportar manualmente em 512, 192, 180, 64 e 48 px.

## 2. Logo horizontal

```text
Create a clean horizontal logo lockup for PrescriMed. Use the approved simple P and Rx monogram on the left and the exact word PrescriMed on the right. Modern clinical sans-serif typography, highly legible, deep navy and clinical blue with one restrained emerald accent. Flat vector identity, transparent background, generous clear space. No tagline, no mockup, no medical cross, no shield, no heartbeat line, no stethoscope, no extra symbols, no alternative spellings, no plus sign.
```

Conferir manualmente a grafia. Se o texto vier errado, manter apenas o símbolo e compor o wordmark em Figma/Stitch.

## 3. Imagem social e Open Graph

```text
Create a premium 1200x630 social sharing image for PrescriMed, a fast medical prescription web app for Brazilian emergency physicians. Clean clinical visual system using deep navy, clinical blue, emerald and soft neutral background. Leave the left 45 percent visually quiet for real headline text to be added later. On the right, show an abstract editable prescription workflow made of clean cards and subtle medical data patterns, without readable patient names, dosages, metrics, testimonials or invented UI copy. Calm, trustworthy, modern, high contrast, no stock-photo doctor, no hospital command center, no medical cross, no shield, no fake dashboard, no embedded words or logo.
```

Adicionar logo e headline depois em ferramenta de design, nunca dentro da geração.

## 4. Ilustração do hero

```text
Create a transparent-background hero illustration for a fast medical prescription web app. Show an abstract three-step flow: pathology selection, treatment items, reviewed prescription document. Minimal layered cards, clean vector geometry, subtle depth, deep navy, clinical blue and emerald accents. No readable text, no patient data, no dosages, no fake metrics, no device mockup, no doctor photo, no hospital dashboard, no cross, no shield, no AI brain, no glow-heavy futuristic style. Designed to sit on the right side of a responsive landing-page hero.
```

## Checklist de aceite

- Reconhecível em 24 px.
- Sem texto gerado incorretamente.
- Sem função ou dado fictício.
- Uma única identidade visual.
- Fundo transparente onde solicitado.
- Arquivo mestre preservado antes dos redimensionamentos.
